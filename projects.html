Project 01

Mboa Health

A health application for Cameroon, built for the phones and networks people actually have.

Problem. Getting to a clinic often means not knowing which one is open, how far it is, or what to do first. Paper records go missing between visits. In an emergency there is no fast way to reach the right contact.

My role. Technical build across the Flutter client and the PHP API.

Approach. A feature-first module layout — auth, profile, records, reminders, notifications, clinic locator, emergency — each owning its own state through Provider. A custom design system called Clinical Sanctuary defines the palette, an 8pt spacing grid, and tonal surface elevation instead of drop shadows, because shadows read badly on cheap LCD panels.

Testing. 88 automated tests: 72 in Flutter and 16 on the PHP API. CI runs lint and the full suite, and blocks a merge on either failing. The repository also carries an architecture document, a deployment runbook and a risk register.

On the symptom checker. It is a rule-based expert system over a decision tree. It is not a model, and it is not AI. Calling it that would misrepresent both the engineering and the clinical risk.

Project 02

Rise for Impact

Founder and Team Lead of a youth initiative — and the person who builds its software.

What it is. A youth initiative running programmes in Cameroon, Ghana, Nigeria and Rwanda. I founded it in April 2025 and lead the team.

What I built. The public site and the application behind it, from schema through API to interface. A content area so programme staff can publish without a developer. Fellowship application handling, from submission through review. An admin view for tracking programmes across the four countries. The deployment pipeline, GitHub to Vercel, with preview builds per branch.

Constraints. Most people arrive on mid-range Android phones over mobile data, so page weight and round trips mattered more than anything else. Programme staff are volunteers, not operators — anything that needed a deploy to change was a design failure.

Reach. The organisation publishes 500+ young people engaged across its programmes. That is programme reach, not a platform user count, and the two should not be confused.

Project 03

AgriLink Ghana

An agricultural marketplace on a PHP MVC framework I wrote rather than installed.

Connects farmers, buyers and transport providers across four role-based portals — produce listings, price bidding, delivery tracking and an analytics view. Built for CS415, a software engineering course, with the full engineering process documented alongside the code.

The interesting part is the framework underneath: a front controller, a routing layer, and the design patterns wired by hand. It is coursework, and labelling it anything else would be dishonest — but the test suite runs if you clone it, which is more than most portfolio projects can say.
