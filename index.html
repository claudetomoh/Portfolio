About Me

Engineering Tomorrow While Empowering Today.

AI engineer building production systems that create measurable impact. Founder of Rise for Impact—a Pan-African platform reaching 500+ changemakers across 4 countries. Deployed 5 ML models during internship: churn prediction (87.3% accuracy), sales forecasting, and conversational AI (92% intent recognition) now live in production.

Technical stack: Python (XGBoost, Prophet, scikit-learn), TypeScript/React, Next.js, PostgreSQL. Full-stack engineer who ships—from model training (hyperparameter tuning, feature engineering) to production deployment (Docker, CI/CD, monitoring). Currently Peer Coach Captain at Ashesi University and Code4All Program Coordinator, empowering 50+ girls in tech through hands-on workshops.

Not interested in building for the sake of building. Every project must move people forward—whether it's connecting farmers to markets (AgriLink), preventing student churn, or creating mentorship at scale. Engineering excellence meets strategic impact.
