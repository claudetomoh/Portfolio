Where intelligent systems meet institutional reality.

Two pieces of work, in different fields, with the same underlying question: what has to be true of a country, an institution or a programme before a technology is any use to it?

01 — AI & space sustainability

Sustainability readiness for small satellite programmes.

A co-authored framework for assessing how ready small satellite programmes in emerging nations are to operate sustainably, and where AI-supported tooling could help.

Small satellites have put orbit within reach of universities and emerging national programmes. The research asks what sustainable participation should look like for programmes that do not have an established space agency's infrastructure or budget.
